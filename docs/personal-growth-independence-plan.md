# Personal Growth & Independence Plan

**Baseline:** Targets are provisional until health, household financial and business metrics are collected.

## P1–P6 vision
- **P1 Health:** Build sustainable routines for sleep, activity, food and recovery.
- **P2 Family:** Schedule and protect meaningful time, with devices/work removed.
- **P3 Freedom:** Leave full-time employment only after financial exit conditions are met. Run LHS for approximately five years, then step back from daily operations while retaining shares.
- **P4 Business:** Ship LHS, onboard real customers and merchants, and establish recurring/repeatable revenue.
- **P5 Wealth:** Strengthen pension, savings, diversified investments and LHS equity.
- **P6 Growth:** Develop prioritisation, sales, leadership, finance, governance and succession skills.

## First year: four quarterly milestones
| Period | LHS milestone | Growth focus |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Complete critical customer journeys, fix blockers, process first genuine transactions | Prioritisation |
| Q2 | Measure active users, retailers, orders, revenue, repeat purchases and retention | Sales and discovery |
| Q3 | Demonstrate repeatable transactions; automate/delegate founder-dependent tasks | Delegation |
| Q4 | Evidence-based decision to scale, correct or reconsider the current model | Finance and management |

## Health
Track H1 sleep consistency, H2 exercise sessions, H3 daily movement, H4 nutrition habits, H5 recovery. Personal targets require baseline and individual suitability.

## Family
Track F1 protected periods kept, F2 partner time, F3 work-free activities, F4 holidays/breaks, F5 genuine presence.

## Employment exit criteria
| Ref | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| E1 | Household emergency reserve |
| E2 | Viable pension trajectory |
| E3 | Sustained LHS revenue |
| E4 | Known personal income needs |
| E5 | Sufficient LHS operating runway |
| E6 | Ability to withstand significant business downturn |
| E7 | Family agreement |
| E8 | Clear business case for leaving employment |

Do not resign based on a calendar milestone alone.

## Founder transition
1. Year 1: Founder/builder, validate revenue.
2. Year 2: Founder/CEO, establish commercial operations.
3. Year 3: CEO/leader, empower functional heads.
4. Year 4: Strategic CEO, test operations without founder intervention.
5. Year 5: Owner, chair, adviser or shareholder role chosen based on company readiness.

## Financial scenarios
- W1: LHS fails; household and retirement security survive.
- W2: LHS becomes a sustainable small or medium business.
- W3: LHS becomes a significant scalable platform.

The retirement plan must not require W3.

## Weekly review
Score each domain P1–P6 as 0 missed, 1 partial, 2 achieved; total /12. Report verified progress, drift, top risk, three next outcomes and decisions requiring approval. A missing score is not zero: report it as unreported.

## First 30 days
A1 establish health baseline and three sustainable habits; A2 protect weekly family periods; A3 limit LHS scope to launch-critical customer transactions; A4 gather financial baseline; A5 practise founder prioritisation; A6 review scores each Sunday.

## Next calculation
Determine the **Employment Exit Number** using household expenditure, required personal income, pension contributions, savings and investments, LHS cash flow/runway and safety margin. Keep financial inputs and individual outcomes in private storage, not this public repository.
