# DSDM — Products (Artefacts)

DSDM tracks progress through the **delivery of products** (artefacts), not
through activity for its own sake (principle 8 — *demonstrate control*).
Products are deliberately **lean**: produce enough to communicate and control,
and no more.

Products are mapped to the lifecycle phase in which they are typically created
or matured. Many evolve continuously rather than being "done" once.

## Products by phase

### Pre-Project
| Product | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Terms of Reference** | High-level statement of the objective and justification for starting the project. |

### Feasibility
| Product | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Feasibility Assessment** | First-cut view of whether the project is technically feasible and financially viable. May be a document, a model, or a prototype. |
| **Outline Plan** | High-level plan for the project. |

### Foundations
| Product | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Business Case** | The justification — costs, benefits, and the business rationale. Baselined here, revisited later. |
| **Prioritised Requirements List (PRL)** | High-level list of requirements, each MoSCoW-prioritised. The scope baseline. |
| **Solution Architecture Definition (SAD)** | High-level design of the business + technical solution. |
| **Development Approach Definition (DAD)** | How quality, testing, and the technical build approach will be handled. |
| **Delivery Plan** | Schedule of timeboxes and increments at a high level. |
| **Management Approach Definition (MAD)** | How the project will be organised, governed, and controlled. |

### Evolutionary Development
| Product | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Evolving Solution** | The developing product itself — including any models, prototypes, and supporting assets. The primary product. |
| **Timebox Plan** | Detailed plan for a single timebox. |
| **Timebox Review Record** | Captures feedback and outcomes from each timebox's review. |

### Deployment
| Product | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Deployed Solution** | The increment released into operational use. |
| **Project Review Report** | Confirms what was delivered vs. expected, and captures lessons. Updated each deployment. |

### Post-Project
| Product | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Benefits Assessment** | Confirms whether the expected business benefits were actually realised once the solution has been in use. |

## Product evolution at a glance

```
                              Delivery Plan / MAD  Record             Review Report
```

## A note on documentation philosophy

DSDM is *not* anti-documentation — it is **anti-waste**. The test for any
product is:

> *"Does this artefact help us deliver the right solution, on time, with
> confidence and control?"*

If yes, produce just enough of it. If no, don't. Models and prototypes often
communicate more effectively (and more cheaply) than long documents, so prefer
them where they fit.
